Student recurrently mixes up which partial derivatives to use in line-integral problems (List_9 Q4–Q5): wrote P_x = Q_y for the conservative test instead of P_y = Q_x, and confuses ∮=0 from a conservative field vs. from a symmetric region.

**Why:** Came up repeatedly in one session — the "cross" partials (P_y, Q_x) are non-intuitive vs. differentiating each by its own variable.

**How to apply:** When helping with Green's theorem or conservative fields, proactively restate: P multiplies dx, Q multiplies dy; both tests use P_y and Q_x (never P_x/Q_y); Green's integrand = Q_x − P_y; ∮=0 can be conservativeness OR region symmetry. Full note pinned in theorems.md §9. See also [[feedback_teaching]] (bracket substituted expressions to avoid sign slips).
